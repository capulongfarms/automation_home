Zones per cycle
How many zones open together in one cycle before handing over make-before-break to the next group. Saves immediately — no Save button. A job of two or more zones never runs fewer than two at a time whatever this says: one open valve is one point of failure between a running pump and a closed system, so 1 only ever applies to a single-zone job.

    Max zones open at once —
    Open zones share the pump's flow, so each one drops below its design pressure — and these solenoids hold current the whole time they are open. The floor is 2, not 0. "No limit" is gone: a second open zone is the relief path if the first valve fails to open physically, and a make-before-break handover needs two open at once. Keep this at twice the Headend's zones per cycle or handovers cannot overlap.
