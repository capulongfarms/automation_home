A CT clamp cannot tell import from export — it measures the size of the current and nothing else, so 5 A going out to the grid reads the same as 5 A coming in. The clamp therefore goes on the house load feed, downstream of the solar tie-in, and measures consumption only; generation comes from the inverter and surplus is the subtraction. House load is VA, not W: mains voltage and power factor are not measured. Good enough to decide whether to run a load, wrong for billing. Leave the URL blank to feed generation in by writing solarGenerationW instead. Either way it must keep arriving — a figure older than 2½ minutes is treated as unknown, and unknown switches the load off, never on.
